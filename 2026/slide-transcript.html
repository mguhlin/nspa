19. Policy language people can use

Policy language people can use

Illustrative applicant guidance for local adaptation

UNCLEAR

“AI-generated

applications may

be disqualified.”

MORE SPECIFIC

You may use AI to brainstorm, outline, or proofread. Write your own statement and verify every claim. Disclose the tool and type of help. Staff review concerns under the published rules. A detector score alone will not decide eligibility. You may request reconsideration.

Local choices: translation, disclosure method, review contact, and deadlines.

21. Sample response for applicants

POLICY DRAFTING LAB SAMPLE 1 OF 3

Sample response: Applicants

SAMPLE RESPONSE

WHY IT WORKS

You may use AI to brainstorm, outline, or proofread, but you must write your own statement, verify each claim, and disclose the tool and type of help.

Allowed help: Names brainstorming, outlining, and proofreading.

Truthful work: Requires the applicant’s own statement and verification of every claim.

Disclosure: Identifies the tool and the type of help received.

Local choice: Specify where and when applicants disclose AI help.

22. Sample response for reviewers

POLICY DRAFTING LAB SAMPLE 2 OF 3

Sample response: Reviewers

SAMPLE RESPONSE

WHY IT WORKS

Use only the approved service for evidence extraction, and the assigned reviewer must verify each quote and source before the committee acts.

Approved use: Limits the task to evidence extraction in an approved service.

Evidence check: Requires verification of each quote and its source.

Accountability: Names the assigned reviewer and sets the check before committee action.

Local choice: Name the approved service and assign the reviewer.

23. Sample response for concerns

POLICY DRAFTING LAB SAMPLE 3 OF 3

Sample response: Concerns

SAMPLE RESPONSE

WHY IT WORKS

Staff review the evidence and published rule, invite clarification when appropriate, and provide a contact for reconsideration.

Evidence review: Connects the concern to evidence and a published rule.

Clarification: Gives the applicant a chance to explain when appropriate.

Reconsideration: Provides a contact for requesting another review.

Local choice: Identify the contact, review steps, and response timeline.

26. Take-home review prompt

Take-home review prompt

For fictional or explicitly approved, minimized information only

TASK AND SOURCE

Using only the supplied excerpt and published rubric,

compare the evidence with one criterion.

OUTPUT

Return the criterion, an exact quote, its source location,

missing evidence, and one neutral follow-up question.

BOUNDARY

Do not invent facts, infer traits, rank applicants, or recommend awards.
