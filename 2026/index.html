My success criteria

Read the success criteria as text

What am I learning today?

I am learning how AI can support scholarship teams and applicants while people protect information, verify evidence, and make decisions.

Why am I learning this?

Clear rules and evidence checks help me protect applicant trust, treat people fairly, and explain scholarship decisions.

How will I know I learned it?

I can:

  • Write a prompt with a task, approved inputs, the actual rubric, boundaries, and checkable output.
  • Verify document flags, rubric evidence, and reviewer handoffs against their sources.
  • Name who approves inputs, checks outputs, and makes the final decision.
  • Check tool approval, data protection, and fairness before real use.
  • Explain applicant AI rules and respond fairly when an essay is flagged.
  • Plan one limited pilot with a human safeguard, an owner, and a review date.
