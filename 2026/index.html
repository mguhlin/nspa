Trust, Transparency, and AI: Building Responsible Scholarship Review Practices

AI can sort, summarize, and suggest. People decide.

Explore both sides of scholarship review: AI supporting your team and AI supporting applicants. Work through three prepared demonstrations, compare policy choices, and plan a small pilot with human checks.

24 core slides and four backup references. All practice records and displayed responses are fictional teaching examples. No AI account is required.
