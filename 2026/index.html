Trust, Transparency, and AI: Building Responsible Scholarship Review Practices

AI can sort, summarize, and suggest. People decide.

I can explore both sides of scholarship review: AI supporting my team and AI supporting applicants. I will examine three prepared demonstrations, compare policy choices, and plan a small pilot with staff verification.

24 core slides and four backup references. My companion and workbook include all case information, worked examples, and note spaces. No AI account is required.
