# Fictional Community contribution rubric

- Anchor 0: No relevant activity described.
- Anchor 1: Activity named, but applicant role unclear.
- Anchor 2: Specific applicant contribution.
- Anchor 3: Specific contribution and a supported result.

Use contribution evidence, not writing polish. Scores in this exercise are provisional. A human reviewer verifies quotations, applies the rubric, and owns the final score.
