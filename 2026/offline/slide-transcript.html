    16. A detector result needs context

    Optional reference D. A signal cannot settle a policy question.

    • What the indicator describes: Flagged qualifying prose, not a misconduct probability.
    • What the studies establish: Findings depend on the language, sample, and detector.
    • What people must decide: The published rule, evidence, and a fair response.

    Your move: Never deny an award using a detector score alone.
