Session overview

Trust, Transparency, and AI

As generative AI becomes an increasingly common tool for both scholarship providers and applicants, organizations are navigating a new balancing act: leveraging AI to improve the efficiency and consistency of application reviews while preserving fairness, authenticity, and trust in the selection process. This interactive workshop explores both sides of that equation, providing practical guidance for integrating AI into scholarship review workflows without compromising privacy, transparency, or sound decision-making.

Participants will revisit the fundamentals of effective AI prompting and learn how well-designed prompts, clear evaluation criteria, and structured workflows can produce more reliable and consistent results. Through live demonstrations, attendees will see how AI can support administrative review tasks, rubric-based scoring, and application organization while maintaining appropriate safeguards for applicant data and human oversight.

The session will also examine one of the most pressing questions facing scholarship providers today: how should organizations respond to applicant use of generative AI? Participants will explore the capabilities and limitations of current AI detection tools, discuss the risks of false positives, and consider practical policy approaches that emphasize fairness, transparency, and authentic evaluation. Attendees will leave with realistic expectations for AI's role in scholarship review and practical frameworks they can adapt within their own organizations.

Takeaways

  • Build structured AI prompts and evaluation frameworks that improve consistency, reduce bias, and support responsible scholarship application reviews.
  • Integrate AI into administrative review processes while protecting applicant privacy, maintaining data security, and preserving human oversight.
  • Evaluate applicant use of generative AI with a balanced understanding of AI detection limitations and develop fair, transparent organizational policies for authentic application assessment.
