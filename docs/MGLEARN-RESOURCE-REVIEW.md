# mglearn resource review

Reviewed September 24, 2026. Inventoried the public repository list and recursive trees, then searched indexed code for NSPA, scholarship, and ethics. Most NSPA hits were shared navigation/footer references, not separate NSPA teaching materials.

## Included and consolidated

- `mglearn/tcea/custom-instructions-library/#ethical-model` and `#ethical-ai`: combined the ETHICAL decision sequence and applicable safeguards into `resources/ethics-toolkit.html`. Removed unsupported assurances about prompt enforcement and model confidence labels. Existing NSPA prompting and policy activities are linked instead of duplicated.
- `mglearn/eles/activities/ai-ethics-case-court.html`: integrated its discussion structure, seven review principles, and decision record into the same toolkit. The three fictional scholarship cases and 20-minute activity are explicitly labeled adaptations of the original 70-minute education activity.
- `mglearn/tcea/protect_rubric_v2/`: linked the canonical online tool from the new Ethics & privacy library tab. A distinct offline manual worksheet lives at `2026/offline/resources/protect/index.html`, with the seven categories, adapted scholarship review questions, evidence/gap fields, JSON restore, text/CSV export, and browser printing. No AI settings, keyword auto-scoring, automatic approval recommendation, CDN export libraries, or network requests. Legal anchors and applicability claims were not imported into the worksheet. Its source category snapshot is retained under `scripts/workshop/offline-inputs/`.

## Reviewed but not added

- `tcea/checklist/` and `tcea/ai-adoption-checklist/`: substantially duplicate K–16 adoption checklists; overlap existing NSPA pilot planning and capacity materials. Neither became another library card.
- `tcea/custom-instructions-library/` selection spreadsheet analysis: overlaps reviewer calibration; fixed correlation thresholds and ranking instructions are not carried into the workshop.
- `tcea/alf/` and `tcea/spr/`: primarily education frameworks or overlapping capacity indicators.
- `tcea/privacy-audit/`: family device permissions, not scholarship-office review.
- `activities/plotpoint/ethics/`: Aristotle literature learning room, not scholarship AI ethics.
- `legalai/`: profession-specific legal training; not transplanted to scholarship work.

The library now has one consolidated ethics toolkit and one canonical privacy review link. Both have usable offline counterparts. Shared mglearn originals were not moved or rewritten because other audiences use them.
