# Design direction

The person affected by a decision anchors the experience. The homepage pairs a large, left-aligned title with Sarah facing an interrupted application. Mission briefings use the same division; investigation rounds replace the photograph with a full-width question and two large choices.

Core tokens: navy `#082759`, midnight `#071F4A`, red `#922B36`, blue `#0A3476`, pale blue `#EEF4FC`, gold `#F5AA0A`, and white. Red and blue identify the perspective, not correctness. Gold marks practical reflection and key discoveries.

Atkinson Hyperlegible is hosted locally for legible letterforms at distance. Responsive type prioritizes the central question, scenario text, and choices. Spacious controls and pale evidence panels carry the interface; there are no scores, speed rankings, or decorative animations. A short user-triggered clue fade respects reduced motion.

The initial layout sketch was a left narrative and right human scene for briefings, followed by a single question, a pair of large choices, investigation tools, and a shared control strip. Review against the brief removed a conventional multi-card dashboard in favor of one team perspective at a time. Debrief content uses five reveal screens rather than shrinking lists into columns. At the smallest projector size, spacing adjusts before important reading text does.

The introduction webdeck uses the existing Web Deck v5 system with the lab palette and generated photographs. It is self-contained, including images, fonts, framework, and speaker notes.
